## 📂 Document Upload & Processing

### Supported File Types
- **PDF Files**: Textbooks, research papers, course materials, lesson plans
- **DOCX Files**: Microsoft Word documents, syllabi, curriculum guides

### Upload Best Practices
- **Clear Structure**: Documents with clear headings and sections work best
- **Readable Text**: Ensure text is not heavily stylized or in complex layouts
- **Size Considerations**: Larger documents (50+ pages) may take longer to process
- **Content Quality**: Well-organized educational content produces better results

### Content Type Selection
- **"Book"**: Choose for textbooks, manuals, or structured educational materials with chapters
- **"Syllabus"**: Select for course outlines, curriculum guides, or single-topic documents

## 🎯 Content Generation Features

### 1. Lesson Plans
**What You Get:**
- Comprehensive lesson structure with clear objectives
- Material lists and preparation requirements
- Step-by-step instructional procedures
- Assessment and evaluation methods
- Conclusion and extension activities

**Best For:**
- K-12 teachers planning daily lessons
- University instructors creating lecture outlines
- Corporate trainers developing workshops
- Homeschool educators structuring activities

**Example Output:**
```
Title: Introduction to Photosynthesis
Grade Level: 7th Grade Science
Duration: 45 minutes

Learning Objectives:
- Students will understand the basic process of photosynthesis
- Students will identify the inputs and outputs of photosynthesis
- Students will explain the importance of photosynthesis in ecosystems

Materials:
- Microscopes and plant leaf samples
- Photosynthesis diagram handouts
- Colored pencils for labeling

Procedure:
[Detailed step-by-step instruction]
```

### 2. Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)
**What You Get:**
- Diverse question types testing different cognitive levels
- Clear, unambiguous answer choices
- Questions covering key concepts from the content
- Appropriate difficulty level for the target audience

**Best For:**
- Creating quick assessments and quizzes
- Standardized test preparation
- Self-assessment tools for students
- Formative evaluation during lessons

**Tips for Better MCQs:**
- Review questions for clarity and accuracy
- Ensure answer choices are plausible
- Mix easy, medium, and challenging questions
- Check that questions align with learning objectives

### 3. Short Answer Questions
**What You Get:**
- Open-ended questions promoting critical thinking

- Varied question types (define, explain, compare, analyze)
- Questions that encourage deeper understanding

**Best For:**
- Encouraging student reflection and analysis
- Assessing understanding beyond memorization
- Creating discussion prompts
- Developing writing and communication skills

### 4. Content Summaries
**What You Get:**
- Concise overview of main concepts
- Key points and essential information
- Logical organization of complex topics
- Easy-to-understand language

**Best For:**
- Student study guides and review materials

### 5. Keyword Extraction
**What You Get:**

- Subject-specific vocabulary highlighted

**Best For:**

## 💬 Using the Curriculens Chatbot

### Getting Started with Chat
- Ask specific questions about uploaded content
- Select specific chapters for focused discussions
- Use natural language - no special commands needed
- Build conversations by asking follow-up questions

### Effective Question Types

**Clarification Questions:**
- "Can you explain photosynthesis in simpler terms?"
- "What's the difference between mitosis and meiosis?"
- "How does this concept relate to real-world applications?"

**Teaching Strategy Questions:**
- "How can I make this topic more engaging for 8th graders?"
- "What hands-on activities would work well for this lesson?"
- "How can I differentiate this content for various skill levels?"

**Assessment Questions:**
- "What are some good discussion questions for this chapter?"
- "How can I assess student understanding of this concept?"
- "What are common misconceptions about this topic?"

**Content Expansion:**
- "Can you provide more examples of this concept?"
- "What are some related topics I should cover?"
- "How does this connect to previous lessons?"

### Chat Tips for Maximum Benefit
- Be specific about what you're looking for
- Reference specific chapters or sections when relevant
- Ask for examples, analogies, or real-world connections
- Request different perspectives or approaches
- Save useful conversations by exporting chat history

## 🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Upload Problems
- **File Too Large**: Break large documents into smaller sections
- **Text Not Recognized**: Check that PDF files contain selectable text, not just images
- **Processing Errors**: Try uploading a different section of the document

### Generation Issues
- **Incomplete Output**: Try generating content for smaller sections
- **Off-Topic Results**: Ensure uploaded content is focused and educational
- **Poor Quality**: Use higher-quality source materials for better results

### Export Problems
- **Download Failures**: Check your browser's download settings
- **Formatting Issues**: Try a different export format (PDF vs. Word)
- **Missing Content**: Verify all generated content is visible before exporting
