# 🤖 Curriculens AI Assistant Capabilities

## What is Curriculens?

Curriculens is an intelligent AI-powered educational assistant designed to revolutionize curriculum design and educational content creation. It serves as your personal teaching assistant, capable of transforming raw educational materials into structured, pedagogically sound learning resources.

---

## 🎯 Core Capabilities

### 1. **Document Intelligence & Processing**
- **PDF & DOCX Parsing**: Automatically extracts and processes text from uploaded PDF and Microsoft Word documents
- **Chapter Detection**: Intelligently identifies and organizes book chapters, sections, and learning modules
- **Content Structuring**: Breaks down large documents into manageable, topic-focused segments
- **Keyword Extraction**: Identifies key terms and concepts using advanced NLP techniques

### 2. **AI-Powered Content Generation**
- **Lesson Plans**: Creates comprehensive, structured lesson plans with objectives, materials, procedures, and assessments
- **Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)**: Generates diverse, challenging multiple-choice questions for assessments
- **Short Answer Questions**: Develops targeted short-response questions for deeper learning evaluation
- **Content Summaries**: Produces concise, accurate summaries of complex educational materials
- **Learning Objectives**: Formulates clear, measurable learning outcomes aligned with educational standards

### 3. **Interactive Learning Assistant**
- **Curriculens Chatbot**: Provides instant, context-aware answers to questions about uploaded content
- **Chapter-Specific Queries**: Allows focused discussions on specific chapters or sections
- **Educational Guidance**: Offers pedagogical advice and teaching strategies
- **Concept Clarification**: Explains complex topics in simple, understandable terms

### 4. **Export & Sharing Capabilities**
- **PDF Export**: Download generated content as professionally formatted PDF documents
- **Word Document Export**: Save materials as editable Microsoft Word files
- **Chat History Export**: Preserve and share valuable Q&A sessions
- **Batch Content Export**: Export multiple generated materials simultaneously

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Upload Your Content**: Simply drag and drop PDF or DOCX files
2. **Select Content Type**: Choose between "Syllabus" or "Book" for optimal processing
3. **Explore Chapters**: Review automatically detected sections and topics
4. **Generate Materials**: Create lesson plans, questions, or summaries with one click
5. **Chat & Learn**: Ask questions about your content using the built-in chatbot
6. **Export & Share**: Download your materials in your preferred format
